# Project overview

> source: `README.md`, `hugo.toml`, `content/posts/en/Sytrism as a new form of government.en.md`

Mind Journal is a personal blog about political philosophy, alternative forms
of governance, Sytrism, direct democracy, technology, AI, and networked
communities. The repository publishes English and Russian content through
Hugo. (`README.md`; `hugo.toml`)

The project describes Sytrism as a proposed politico-philosophical model based
on balancing human institutions, artificial intelligence, and cyber-networks.
The current articles present this as an idea under exploration, not as a
validated political science result. (`content/posts/en/Sytrism as a new form of government.en.md`)

Public site: <https://mmortall.github.io/blogs-Mind-Journal/>.

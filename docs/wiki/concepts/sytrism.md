# Sytrism

> source: `content/posts/en/Sytrism as a new form of government.en.md`, `content/posts/ru/Сайтризм как новая форма правления.ru.md`, `content/posts/en/Sytrizm and the ideas of Jacques Fresco.en.md`

Sytrism is presented in this repository as a proposed model of governance based
on three sources of power:

1. human institutions, representing culture, ethics, and responsibility;
2. artificial intelligence, representing analysis, forecasting, and efficiency;
3. cyber-networks or direct-democratic collective intelligence, representing
   distributed participation and public will.

The articles propose unanimous decisions, a two-of-three compromise, and
domain-specific vetoes as possible operating rules. They also compare the idea
with Jacques Fresco’s technology-oriented governance and resource-based
economy. These are project concepts and comparisons, not independently verified
claims. (`content/posts/en/Sytrism as a new form of government.en.md`;
`content/posts/en/Sytrizm and the ideas of Jacques Fresco.en.md`)

Related topics include the freedom/order tension, the social contract, direct
democracy, and institutional safeguards for preserving core principles.
(`content/posts/en/About the essence of any political system.en.md`;
`content/posts/en/Direct democracy.en.md`;
`content/posts/en/About a possible linchpin for Sightrism.en.md`)

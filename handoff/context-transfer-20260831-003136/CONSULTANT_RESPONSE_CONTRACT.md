## Evidence rules

Use `[REQ]`, `[FACT]`, `[OBS]`, `[DEC]`, `[INF]`, `[HYP]`, `[UNK]`, and `[CONFLICT]`. Give High/Medium/Low confidence with reasons. External research normally cannot exceed project evidence level `L0` without local review.

## Failure conditions

The return fails if it:

- skips canonical practice before node ranking;
- presents the project-local axes as universal political truth;
- assigns complete ideologies to people from one project;
- conflates software capability, movement rhetoric, institutional authority, deployment, and effectiveness;
- treats a working URL as proof of current activity;
- uses Wikipedia or official marketing as sole load-bearing evidence;
- lacks current access dates and counterevidence;
- satisfies breadth with cosmetic variants;
- returns one giant file, non-Markdown output, or claims local work without access.

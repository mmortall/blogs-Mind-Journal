This is a working world map of direct critics of The Venus Project, adjacent political thinkers, civic organisations, and open governance tools. It does not claim that all participants agree or that every relationship has been independently audited.

Only public original materials are included. Confidential reports, private correspondence, and unverified accounts are deliberately left out.

The filters preserve three distinctions that are easy to lose. A direct critic comments on The Venus Project itself; an adjacent author addresses a similar problem without belonging to that dispute. A deliberation platform helps a group understand opinions; it is not automatically a secure election system. Finally, open source makes code inspectable, but does not make the institution democratic, representative, or safe.

Each entry therefore records not only an idea, but also what it proposes instead, its present status, and a serious caveat. Inclusion is not endorsement.
